Solution 1 — Rice-Fish Farming

A self-sustaining loop: less fresh water needed, no bought fertilizer or pesticide required.

A flooded rice paddy stocked with fish — an age-old, FAO-recognized farming system.

01
Fish are released into the flooded paddy field
02
Fish eat parasites & dried leaves — cleaning the water naturally
03
Fish draw oxygen from the water soaked up by the rice plant
04
Fish waste becomes natural fertilizer for the growing rice

Results = Impact

More Food

Two harvests from one field — rice and fish together.

Less Money

Lower input costs — far less spent on store-bought fertilizer.

Free Fertilizer

Fish waste naturally enriches the soil.

Water Conservation

Natural filtration by fish means less fresh water pumped.

Solution 2 — Solar Grazing on Barren Land

Wasted cleaning water becomes new pasture — clean energy and clean land from the same panels.

01
Solar panels installed on barren / deserted land
02
Regular cleaning water seeps into the soil beneath the panels
03
Seeds sown under the panels sprout using that run-off water
04
Once grown, sheep graze the land for months

Sheep grazing under a solar array — the agrivoltaics model already used worldwide.

Results = Impact

Clean Energy

Large-scale solar generation on previously barren land.

Water Management

Water used to clean panels is reused for irrigation.

Clean Environment

Barren land is transformed into living, green pasture.

Cheap Fodder

Self-seeded grass feeds grazing livestock at virtually no cost.

Cattle Farming

Indirectly, more food on the table through livestock.

Solution 3 — Green Hydrogen Fuel

Fun fact: the only waste this engine produces is water — nothing else!

A solar-powered electrolyzer — splitting water into hydrogen and oxygen fuel.

01
Clean energy generated by EarthWise solar panels
02
Energy powers a hydrogen & oxygen (H₂ / O₂) generator
03
Cheap H₂ and O₂ fuel delivered to hydro-power engines
04
Engines run clean — the only waste produced is water

Results = Impact

Cheap Fuel

Low-cost H₂ and O₂ fuel, produced straight from sunlight and water.

Cleaner Environment

Zero-emission power — engines release no smoke, soot, or air pollution.

Water Recovery

Engine exhaust is pure water, captured on-site and reused for irrigation.
